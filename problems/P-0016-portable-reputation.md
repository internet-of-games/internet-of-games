# P-0016: Portable reputation and moderation standing

**In one sentence:** should a player banned for harassment in one game carry that ban into another, and if so, how could that work without creating unaccountable cross-game blacklists?

## Why it matters

There are real arguments on both sides:

- **For:** a known harasser shouldn't get a fresh start in every game, and protecting players (especially children) matters.
- **Against:** moderation decisions are sometimes wrong, report systems get abused, a "permanent record" across all of gaming is disproportionate, and sharing negative data about people raises serious legal issues, including data protection, automated decision-making and defamation.

## What exists today

- **Platform-level bans** already apply across every game on a console network, run by the platform holder.
- **Industry groups** such as the [Fair Play Alliance](https://fairplayalliance.org/) share research on player behavior and healthy communities.
- **No neutral, accountable system** exists for sharing standing between unrelated games.

## Default position

Until research shows otherwise:

1. **Negative signals never travel by default.**
2. **Positive signals can be explored.** For example, a game could issue a "good standing for 12 months" credential that the player *chooses* to show. This is lower risk because the player controls it and it can't be used against them without their knowledge.
3. **Portable *blocks* belong to [P-0018](P-0018-friends-and-presence.md).** A player carrying their own block list protects them without judging anyone else publicly.

## Open questions

- **P-0016-Q1:** Is positive-only reputation useful, or would its absence just become a negative signal in itself?
- **P-0016-Q2:** What do data-protection and defamation law say about sharing moderation outcomes between companies?
- **P-0016-Q3:** How could a "good standing" credential be gamed?
- **P-0016-Q4:** How should child-safety cases be handled, given they go through legal channels rather than game-to-game sharing?

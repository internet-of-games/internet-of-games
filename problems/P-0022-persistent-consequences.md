# P-0022: Persistent consequences across games

**In one sentence:** the vision says actions should matter beyond a single game, so how can facts about what happened in one world shape another, without a shared server or a shared story canon?

## Why it matters

"Be anyone, do anything", with actions that have consequences, is the heart of the long-term [vision](../VISION.md). It's also by far the hardest part.

## What exists today

- **Persistent societies inside one game.** EVE Online's players elect a council every year; 32,675 votes were cast in 2026 ([EVE Online](https://www.eveonline.com/news/view/heres-your-csm-21)). Roleplay servers for GTA V host persistent player-run cities ([Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/business/rockstar-acquires-community-roleplay-team-cfx-re)).
- **Continuity inside a franchise.** Some series import save-game decisions into sequels; Skylanders carries character progress between games ([prior-art.md](../docs/prior-art.md#skylanders)).
- **Shared universes inside one company**, such as the Disney universe being built inside Fortnite ([GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/a-disney-persistent-universe-tied-to-fortnite-is-in-the-works/1100-6520900/)).

## A starting idea: "world facts" as claims

Start with what Phase 1 already makes possible. A game can issue signed facts about what a player *did* there, not just achievements:

> "Founded the team that won the 2027 season of League X."
> "Was elected mayor of Harbor Town."

Other games may weave these facts into their own story, using the interpretation rules in [P-0014](P-0014-claim-interpretation.md). **Each game remains its own canon.** If two games' facts contradict each other, nothing breaks, because each game decides what's true inside it.

## Research questions

- **P-0022-Q1:** What vocabulary would let games express events, roles and relationships?
- **P-0022-Q2:** How should canon conflicts between cooperating games be handled? Could a group of games agree on a shared canon?
- **P-0022-Q3:** How do players consent to their history being used in stories, especially stories other players will see?
- **P-0022-Q4:** Could AI help games weave foreign facts into their narrative safely ([P-0023](P-0023-traveling-ai-characters.md))?

## How to help

- Narrative designers: sketch how a game could use world facts from another game.
- Research how existing games carry choices from one installment to the next.
